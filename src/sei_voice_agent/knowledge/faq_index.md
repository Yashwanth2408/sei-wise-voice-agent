# Wise Transfer Tracking FAQ Index (Compact)


## In-Scope Topics
- Transfer status: how to check in Wise account (Home page)
- Status meanings: Processing, Money received, Transfer sent, Complete
- Why transfer delayed: sender bank slow, compliance checks, wrong recipient details, weekend/holiday, recipient bank processing, slower payment method
- Recipient didn't receive: check sender name (may show Wise or partner), verify amount, reference number, check account currency
- Transfer receipt: sender can download to help recipient's bank locate transfer
- Proof of payment needed: if money didn't reach in expected time, or to verify sender account ownership
- Banking delays on weekends/holidays
- Payment method timelines: card (instant), bank transfer (1-4 days), Swift (1-6 days)


## Out-of-Scope (Deflect)

- Exchange rates / currency conversion

- Account opening / KYC / security
- Cards / account settings / password
- Refunds / disputes / fraud / chargebacks
- Changing recipient details as workflow action
